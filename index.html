Weighted score formula
(Route × 0.40) + (Vendor × 0.20) + (Customer × 0.15) + (Sales × 0.15) + (Scale × 0.05) + (Geo × 0.05)

The app stores raw category scores, weighted score, tier, and confidence separately. Confidence is not blended into score to avoid distorting rank order.

40%

Route-to-Revenue Fit

Primary signal. Rewards partners already selling UC and CX, or those with a credible UC base to expand from.

20%

Vendor Displacement

Looks for legacy or adjacent estates where IPI has a wedge. Vendor score ≤ 2 caps a partner at Tier 2.

15%

Customer & Use Case Fit

Higher scores for contact-centre-heavy and CX-relevant verticals such as healthcare, finance, retail, housing and public sector.

15%

Sales Motion Maturity

Proxy for whether they can actually sell. The current version uses services breadth, size and notes-based signals.

5%

Scale Fit

Prefers partners large enough to execute but not so large they become slow and politically complex.

5%

Geographic Fit

Rewards UK alignment. The current source data is UK-weighted, but the model still surfaces limited or non-UK cases.
